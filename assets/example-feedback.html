Breakdown Opt-in: Design Feedback

Jane Doe · Example Co · "Free 5-minute breakdown" opt-in · Review date: Nov 5, 2026 · Design only (no copy or compliance audit)

Page reviewed: example.com/preview/page (desktop 1440px, mobile 390px)

Overall: a strong, modern page. The dark purple look is consistent, the type pairs well, the sections are clear and the mobile layout works (no sideways scrolling, the testimonials swipe nicely, and every button goes to the form). The main issue is that the form button sits too far down on both desktop and mobile. Most other fixes are spacing and small polish.

What already works (keep it)

  • Clear look: one accent colour, bold display headlines, readable body text, good contrast on dark.
  • Every button goes to the form (#optin) on desktop and mobile.
  • Numbered "you'll discover" cards and the audience pills are easy to scan.
  • Testimonials turn into a swipe row on mobile with a "swipe for more" hint.
  • Earnings and testimonial disclaimers are in place.

Desktop · Hero and form

↑ Back to summary1Form button is below the first screen Change now

On a normal laptop screen (900px tall) visitors see only the First Name field; the button is at about 1,370px. The 4-line headline at ~60px uses most of the first screen.

  • Bring the headline down to about 44–48px and let it run 2–3 lines (widen the text box).
  • Cut the space between the sub-headline and the video/form row.
  • Together with fix 2, aim for the form button inside the first screen, or at least the Email field.
↑ Back to summary2Tighten the form Change now

There are big empty gaps above and below the Privacy / Terms links, so they float in the middle of the form. Remove the extra gap and move that line under the button, next to "Your info is safe. We never sell your data." This also shortens the form by about 100px (helps fix 1 and M1).

↑ Back to summary3Grey square in the email field Change now

Only the email field has an icon, and it shows as a plain grey square (looks broken). Remove it, or use a proper envelope icon in every field so they match.

↑ Back to summary4Box behind the form button Change now

The button sits on a darker rectangle with a gradient whose straight edges show against the card. Remove that wrapper's background so only the button's glow shows.

↑ Back to summary5Empty left column Change now

The left side ends after the 3 bullets while the form keeps going, leaving about 330px of empty space. Fill it with the proof items that sit under the form now (credentials and awards) as a 2×2 block, or one short testimonial. Fix 1 and 2 will also shrink the gap.

↑ Back to summary6Stray space in the top label Change now

The label is built from two pieces of text, which leaves a gap before the comma. Make it one line of text (this also fixes M2).

↑ Back to summary7Video thumbnail Confirm first

The frame catches Jane mid-word with a frown. It's the biggest image on the page and the first time visitors see her. Use a frame where she is smiling and looking at the camera, or a still from her headshot set (assets/headshots/). Confirm which image with the team.

↑ Back to summary8"Featured on" logos Confirm first

The outlets are shown as text in pills, which reads like tags rather than press. Use the real logos in one colour (white or light grey) on a single row. Needs the logo files from Jane's team.

Desktop · "Inside" and "Who it's for"

↑ Back to summary9Testimonial cards are different heights Change now

The three cards end at three different heights (the middle one is tallest, the third shortest). Make all three the same height and keep the name/photo row at the bottom of each card so the names line up.

Desktop · Final call to action and footer

↑ Back to summary10Footer links Change now

Privacy and Terms are only linked inside the form. Add a small link row to the footer: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Earnings Disclaimer.

↑ Back to summary11Brand look Confirm first

Her live consultant funnel (example-funnel.com) uses navy + gold. This page is dark purple. If this opt-in leads into that funnel, the jump in colours may feel like a different company. Confirm with the team that purple is the intended look for this funnel (no change needed if yes).

Mobile · Hero and form

↑ BackM1Headline takes 6 lines Change now

The headline fills most of the first screen and the form button is about 1.6 screens down. Drop the mobile headline to about 30–32px (4 lines) and apply fix 2.

↑ BackM2"The Example Title" is hidden Change now

On mobile the top label shows only "FREE BREAKDOWN FROM JANE DOE". Fix 6 (one line of text) brings it back; let it wrap to 2 lines.

↑ BackM3Jane appears only after the form Change now

On mobile the video moves below the form, so visitors are asked for their details before they see who is teaching. Keep the form first, but add a small round photo of Jane next to the top label (or a short "with Jane Doe" row with photo) so her face is on the first screen.

↑ BackM4Button text wraps Change now

"WATCH THE FREE BREAKDOWN →" breaks onto 2 lines in the form and in the "Inside" section (there the arrow floats far right). Reduce the button font size or letter spacing on mobile so it fits on one line.

Mobile · Proof and sections

↑ BackM5Proof list alignment Change now

The 4 proof items are centered, so the icons don't line up. Show them as a left-aligned list or a 2×2 grid (same block as fix 5).

No other mobile issues OK

No sideways scrolling, buttons are large enough to tap, the testimonial swipe row works, and the final button and footer read well.
